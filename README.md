**🔹 HR Performance Management Dashboard | Power BI**<br>

I recently designed an end-to-end HR Performance Management Dashboard using Power BI, focusing on transforming raw HR data into clear, actionable insights for decision-makers.

**The objective of this dashboard was to answer two critical questions:**<br>
1️⃣ How is the organization performing overall?<br>
2️⃣ What factors are driving that performance?<br>

To achieve this, the dashboard is structured into two dedicated pages, each serving a distinct analytical purpose.

**📄 Page 1 — Performance Overview (Executive View)**<br>
This page provides a high-level snapshot of the organization’s performance, designed for HR managers and leadership to quickly assess the current state.

**🔹 KPI Cards**<br>
The top section highlights key organizational metrics:,<br>
Total Employees – workforce size at a glance<br>
Average Performance Rating – overall performance health<br>
High Performers Percentage – proportion of employees rated ≥ 4<br>
Average Monthly Income – compensation overview<br>
These KPIs enable quick, data-driven evaluation without diving into detailed charts.

**🔹 Performance by Department**<br>
This visualization compares average performance ratings across departments, helping identify:
High-performing departments<br>
Departments that may need performance improvement initiatives<br>

**🔹 Performance Rating Distribution**<br>
The distribution view shows how employees are spread across different performance levels, providing insight into:
Performance balance within the organization<br>
Concentration of high vs average performers

   **🔹Job Satisfaction vs Performance**<br>
This analysis explores the relationship between job satisfaction and performance, offering evidence on whether employee satisfaction aligns with higher performance outcomes.


**📄 Page 2 — Performance Drivers Analysis (Analytical View)**<br>
While Page 1 answers “What is happening?”, Page 2 focuses on “Why is it happening?” by examining the key drivers of performance.

**🔹 Training Impact on Performance**<br>
This visual analyzes how training frequency influences employee performance, helping HR teams evaluate:
Effectiveness of training programs<br>
Return on learning and development initiatives

 **🔹 Career Progression & Role Stability**<br>
By analyzing years in the current role, this chart highlights how career stagnation or role stability affects performance trends over time.

**🔹 Department & Job Role Contribution (Treemap)**<br>
This view breaks down performance contribution across departments and job roles, helping identify:<br>
Roles that drive organizational performance<br>
Areas with high workforce concentration and impact

**🔹 Performance Matrix (Department × Job Level)**<br>
The matrix provides a granular view of average performance across job levels and departments, enabling cross-comparison and deeper workforce insights.

**🎯 Conclusion**<br>
This dashboard demonstrates how HR analytics can support performance management, talent development, and strategic workforce planning through clear visualization and business-focused insights.

**🔧 Tools & Technologies Used**<br>
Power BI – Data modeling, DAX calculations & interactive dashboards<br>
DAX – KPI measures (High Performers %, Average Rating, etc.)<br>
Excel / CSV – Data cleaning & preparation<br>
HR Analytics Concepts – Performance evaluation & workforce insights
